# Block 29 — AI Academy

AI Academy is the learning environment for users, reviewers, developers, and mentors.

## Learning tracks
- AI literacy
- Prompt engineering
- Evidence and source criticism
- Knowledge graph practice
- Mentor Council operation
- Safety and human oversight
- Research methods
- PALACO governance

## Course object
Courses are versioned knowledge objects with modules, learning objectives, source references, exercises, assessments, and completion records.

## Rule
Educational content must distinguish established knowledge, interpretation, hypothesis, and unknowns. Completion does not grant professional licensure or authority.

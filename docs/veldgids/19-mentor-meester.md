# Block 19 — Mentor Meester / Geneesheer-Directeur

The Mentor Meester is the orchestration role for De Mentale Veldgids.

## Responsibilities
1. Interpret the user's question and context.
2. Select relevant permanent mentors.
3. Determine whether freelance expertise is warranted.
4. Dispatch bounded analyses.
5. Compare evidence, uncertainty and disagreement.
6. Request additional review where necessary.
7. Produce an integrated response.
8. Preserve provenance and audit evidence.

## Authority boundary
The Mentor Meester coordinates reasoning; it does not become the user's autonomous decision-maker.

For clinical or safety-critical matters, it must surface uncertainty and route toward appropriate human professional oversight.

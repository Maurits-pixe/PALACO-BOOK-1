# Block 24 — Safety & Human Oversight

## Safety principles
- AI is advisory, not a substitute for licensed professional care.
- No autonomous diagnosis.
- No autonomous emergency intervention.
- High-risk signals trigger explicit safety guidance and human escalation.
- Sensitive data is access-controlled and audited.
- The user can inspect important sources and uncertainty.
- Model output is never treated as authority merely because it was generated by AI.

## Clinical boundary
The label Geneesheer-Directeur describes the product's orchestration role; it does not imply that the AI itself is a licensed physician.
